# Siddur — store listing

**Short description.** A beautiful siddur: Hebrew with synced English, four nusachim, works offline.

**Full description.**

Siddur is a prayer book made for the screen in your hand, and for the one that opens like a book.

Every paragraph pairs its Hebrew with the English translation, aligned side by side so you never lose your place. Read in Hebrew alone, as on paper, and tap any paragraph to reveal its translation; or read both at once. Choose Ashkenaz, Sefard, Ari (Chabad) or Edot HaMizrach.

• All 844 prayers of all four nusachim are built in. No download, no account, no connection needed, ever.
• Today: the Hebrew date, Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh awareness, and the service for this moment. Turn on location for real sunrise and sunset; it never leaves your phone.
• Typography made for prayer: Frank Ruhl, David and Noto Serif Hebrew faces, pinch to resize, line spacing, vowels and cantillation on or off, light, sepia and dark pages.
• Traditional page: bold opening words, quiet rubrics, Hebrew headings between hairlines.
• Bookmarks, continue where you left off, and search by English or Hebrew name.
• Made for foldables: closed, a single clean page; opened, a two-page spread with the translation on the facing page, and a tabletop mode when half open.

Texts courtesy of Sefaria. Hebrew: The Metsudah Siddur (1981). English: based on the Metsudah linear siddur by Avrohom Davis. No ads, no tracking.
