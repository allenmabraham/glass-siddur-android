# Siddur privacy policy

Siddur is a prayer book. It collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

**Location.** If you turn on "Use location for sunrise & sunset" in Settings, the app asks for coarse location permission and reads your approximate position once to compute local sunrise and sunset times. The position is used only on the device, is never stored beyond the current session, and is never sent anywhere. The feature is off by default.

**Network.** Every prayer text ships inside the app, so it works entirely offline. The app contacts Sefaria (sefaria.org) only to fetch a prayer that is somehow missing from the bundle, and that request contains nothing but the name of the prayer.

**Storage on your device.** Your reading preferences (nusach, language, fonts, text size), bookmarks and the last prayer you read are stored on your device only, in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
